    • About Us

    We believe the best buildings and interiors are, at heart, stories.

    Bravyn Built Stories is an independent editorial publication devoted to interior design, residential architecture, and the materials and ideas that shape how we live.

    We publish long-form articles, practical guides, and considered criticism — not trend reports, not product roundups, not sponsored content disguised as journalism. Our contributors include architects, interior designers, material scientists, and writers who care about the built environment. We believe that design knowledge should be accessible, rigorous, and useful.

    The name comes from a simple idea: that buildings and interiors are, at their best, stories. They have characters (the people who live in them), plots (the daily rituals they accommodate), settings (the sites and climates they respond to), and themes (the values of the people who make them). Good design is good storytelling.

    Our Approach

    We are based in London and Lisbon. Our editorial perspective is shaped by a few core beliefs:

    Rigour over trends

    We are less interested in what is fashionable this season than in what has proven to work over decades. We look for principles, not prescriptions.

    Materials matter

    We believe that understanding materials — how they age, how they perform, how they are sourced — is fundamental to making good design decisions.

    Craft and care

    We celebrate the quiet details: the way light falls across a wall, the sound of a door closing, the feel of a stair tread underfoot.

    Accessible knowledge

    We write for people who care about their homes, not just for professionals. Design knowledge should be shared, not gatekept.

    Editorial Independence

    Bravyn Built Stories is reader-supported. We do not accept sponsored posts, advertorials, or paid placements. When we mention products or materials, it is because they are relevant to the story, not because we were paid to include them.

    “A room is not a storage unit for furniture. It is an atmosphere. The question is not what fits, but what belongs.”

    Who We Are

    Bravyn Built Stories is produced by a small editorial team and a network of contributing writers, architects, and researchers.

    Founding Editor

    Elena trained as an architect at the Politecnico di Milano and practiced interior design for twelve years before turning to writing. She is interested in the quiet details of domestic life and writes about design as a daily practice rather than a luxury pursuit.

    James Okafor

    Architecture Editor

    James is a practicing architect and writer based in London. He writes about residential architecture, passive design, and the relationship between buildings and their sites. His work has appeared in The Architects' Journal and RIBA Journal.

    Sofia Lindqvist

    Materials & Craft

    Sofia is a material scientist and maker based in Lisbon. She writes about natural materials, traditional craft techniques, and the science of how things age. She is particularly interested in the beauty of patina and imperfection.

    Marcus Chen

    Contributing Writer

    Marcus is a writer and former interior designer. He covers lighting, space planning, and the psychology of interiors. He is the author of The Well-Lit Room and writes regularly for design publications.
