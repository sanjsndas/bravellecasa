    About Bravyn Built Stories

    Bravyn Built Stories is an independent editorial publication devoted to interior design, residential architecture, and the materials and ideas that shape how we live. We publish long-form articles, practical guides, and considered criticism — not trend reports, not product roundups, not sponsored content disguised as journalism.

    The name comes from a simple idea: that buildings and interiors are, at their best, stories. They have characters (the people who live in them), plots (the daily rituals they accommodate), settings (the sites and climates they respond to), and themes (the values of the people who make them). Good design is good storytelling.

    We are based in London and Lisbon. Our contributors include architects, interior designers, material scientists, and writers who care about the built environment. We believe that design knowledge should be accessible, rigorous, and useful — not exclusive, vague, or precious.

    Editorial Independence

    Bravyn Built Stories is reader-supported. We do not accept sponsored posts, advertorials, or paid placements. When we mention products or materials, it is because they are relevant to the story, not because we were paid to include them.

    Editorial Team

    Elena Marchetti — Founding Editor

    Elena trained as an architect at the Politecnico di Milano and practiced interior design for twelve years before turning to writing. She is interested in the quiet details of domestic life — the way light falls across a wall, the sound of a door closing, the feel of a stair tread underfoot. She writes about design as a daily practice rather than a luxury pursuit.
