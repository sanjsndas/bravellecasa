    Categories

    Every story we publish falls into one of these editorial sections. Each is a lens on the same subject: how spaces are made, and how they shape the way we live.

    Featured Section

    Interior Design

    Rooms that work — and the decisions behind them. We write about layout, colour, texture, furniture, and the quiet details that make a house feel like a home. No trends, no shopping lists. Just considered writing about the spaces we inhabit.

    48 articles
    Section

    Architecture

    Residential buildings, extensions, renovations, and the structural thinking behind them. We look at how architects solve problems of light, space, and site — and what those solutions mean for the people who live there.

    36 articles
    Section

    Materials

    Stone, timber, clay, lime, brass, wool. We trace where materials come from, how they age, and why some surfaces feel better underfoot than others. A section for people who care what things are made of.

    29 articles
    Section

    Lighting

    Natural and artificial light, and how they shape a room. We cover window placement, lamp selection, colour temperature, and the difference between lighting a space and illuminating it.

    22 articles
    Section

    Space Planning

    How rooms connect, where walls go, and why some layouts feel effortless while others fight you. Practical writing about circulation, zoning, storage, and the geometry of daily life.

    18 articles
    Section

    Craft & Detail

    Joinery, plasterwork, metalwork, and the people who do it well. Profiles of makers, close readings of detail, and arguments for why the small things matter more than the big gestures.

    15 articles
    Section

    Sustainability

    Building and renovating with less. We look at passive design, low-carbon materials, retrofit, and the honest trade-offs involved in making a home more environmentally sound.

    Contact

    Editorial inquiries: editor@bravynbuiltstories.com

    Pitches: We accept pitches for long-form articles (1,500–4,000 words) on topics related to our sections. Send a brief outline and writing samples to pitch@bravynbuiltstories.com. We respond within two weeks.

    Corrections: If you spot an error, please let us know at corrections@bravynbuiltstories.com. We take accuracy seriously and will publish corrections promptly.

    Editorial Team

    Elena Marchetti — Founding Editor

    Elena trained as an architect at the Politecnico di Milano and practiced interior design for twelve years before turning to writing. She is interested in the quiet details of domestic life — the way light falls across a wall, the sound of a door closing, the feel of a stair tread underfoot. She writes about design as a daily practice rather than a luxury pursuit.
