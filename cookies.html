    Cookies Policy

    How we use cookies at Bravyn Built Stories — and how you can manage them.

    Last updated: March 2025

    Bravyn Built Stories uses the minimum number of cookies necessary for the site to function properly and to understand how our readers use the site. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not sell any data collected through cookies.

    In short: We use a few essential cookies to make the site work. We do not track you across the internet, and we do not serve targeted ads. You can disable cookies in your browser at any time.

    What Are Cookies?

    Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the site's owners. Cookies do not contain any information that personally identifies you, but personal information that we store about you may be linked, by us, to the information stored in and obtained from cookies.

    How We Use Cookies

    We use cookies for the following purposes:

    • Essential functionality: To remember your category filter preferences during a visit, so you don't have to re-select them on every page. This cookie expires when you close your browser.
    • Analytics (privacy-respecting): To understand which articles are read and how readers navigate the site. This data is aggregated and anonymized. We do not track individual readers across other websites.
    • No advertising cookies: We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. We do not serve targeted ads, and we do not share data with advertising networks.

    Cookies We Use

    The table below lists the specific cookies we use on Bravyn Built Stories:

    Cookie Name Purpose Duration
    bravyn_session Essential — remembers your category filter preferences during a visit. Session (expires when you close your browser)
    bravyn_analytics Analytics — anonymized data on page views and navigation. No personal data stored. 30 days
    bravyn_prefs Essential — stores your cookie consent preference so we don't ask again. 1 year

    Third-Party Cookies

    We do not use third-party cookies for advertising or tracking. If we embed content from other platforms (such as a video or a social media post), those platforms may set their own cookies. We have no control over these cookies. Please check the relevant third-party privacy policies for more information.

    Managing Cookies

    You can control and/or delete cookies as you wish. For details, see aboutcookies.org. You can delete all cookies that are already on your computer and you can set most browsers to prevent them from being placed. If you do this, however, you may have to manually adjust some preferences every time you visit a site, and some services and functionalities may not work.

    Browser-specific instructions:

    • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
    • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
    • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data
    • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Cookies and site data

    Changes to This Policy

    We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will post the updated policy on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

    Contact Us

    If you have any questions about our use of cookies, please contact us at privacy@bravynbuiltstories.com.

    About Bravyn Built Stories

    Bravyn Built Stories is an independent editorial publication devoted to interior design, residential architecture, and the materials and ideas that shape how we live. We publish long-form articles, practical guides, and considered criticism — not trend reports, not product roundups, not sponsored content disguised as journalism.

    The name comes from a simple idea: that buildings and interiors are, at their best, stories. They have characters (the people who live in them), plots (the daily rituals they accommodate), settings (the sites and climates they respond to), and themes (the values of the people who make them). Good design is good storytelling.

    We are based in London and Lisbon. Our contributors include architects, interior designers, material scientists, and writers who care about the built environment. We believe that design knowledge should be accessible, rigorous, and useful — not exclusive, vague, or precious.

    Editorial Independence

    Bravyn Built Stories is reader-supported. We do not accept sponsored posts, advertorials, or paid placements. When we mention products or materials, it is because they are relevant to the story, not because we were paid to include them.

    Editorial Team

    Elena Marchetti — Founding Editor

    Elena trained as an architect at the Politecnico di Milano and practiced interior design for twelve years before turning to writing. She is interested in the quiet details of domestic life — the way light falls across a wall, the sound of a door closing, the feel of a stair tread underfoot. She writes about design as a daily practice rather than a luxury pursuit.

    Contributors

    Bravyn Built Stories works with a small group of contributing writers and researchers, including practicing architects, material specialists, and design historians. Contributor profiles are published alongside their work.
