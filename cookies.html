Cookie Policy

Bravelle Casa Lines uses a minimal set of cookies: only those necessary to keep the site working and to understand readership in an anonymized way. No advertising, no cross-site tracking, no data selling.

Last updated: 12 September 2026 Effective: 12 September 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide information to site owners.

Cookies can be "session cookies" (deleted when you close your browser) or "persistent cookies" (stored for a set period). They can also be "first-party" (set by the site you are visiting) or "third-party" (set by a different domain).

2. Our Approach to Cookies

We are an independent editorial journal, not an advertising business. Our cookie use reflects that:

  • No advertising cookies. We do not serve ads and do not allow ad networks to track you on our site.
  • No cross-site tracking. We do not use cookies to follow you across other websites.
  • Minimal analytics. We use a privacy-conscious analytics tool that does not identify individual readers.
  • No data selling. Cookie data is never sold or shared with data brokers.

We believe you should be able to read about architecture and interiors without being surveilled. Our cookie policy is designed around that principle.

3. Cookies We Use

The table below lists the specific cookies we set, their purpose, and their duration.

3.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the Site to function. They cannot be switched off in our systems. They are usually set in response to actions you take, such as setting your privacy preferences or filling in forms.

Cookie Name Purpose Duration Type
bravelle_consent Stores your cookie consent choice so we don't ask again on every page. 12 months First-party
bravelle_session Maintains basic site functionality during your visit (e.g., form state). Session First-party

3.2 Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how readers use the Site — which articles are read, how visitors arrive, and general device categories. All data is aggregated and anonymized; we cannot identify you personally from this information.

Cookie Name Purpose Duration Type
_pk_id Anonymized visitor identifier used to distinguish unique readers. Contains no personal data. 13 months First-party
_pk_ses Short-lived session cookie used to group page views into a single visit. 30 minutes First-party

Our analytics provider is configured with IP anonymization enabled, meaning your full IP address is never stored. We do not use Google Analytics or any other tool that builds cross-site profiles.

3.3 Functionality Cookies

These are optional cookies that remember choices you make to improve your experience. Currently, we only use one:

Cookie Name Purpose Duration Type
bravelle_theme Remembers your preferred reading mode (light/dark) if you change it. 6 months First-party

4. Third-Party Cookies

We do not set third-party cookies for advertising, remarketing, or social media tracking. If we embed content from external platforms (e.g., a video from Vimeo), those platforms may set their own cookies. Where this occurs, we will note it clearly in the relevant article and, where required, obtain your consent before loading the embed.

5. Managing Cookies

You have several options for controlling or deleting cookies:

  • Browser settings: Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. The "Help" section of your browser will explain how. Note that blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect site functionality.
  • Opt-out of analytics: You can opt out of our anonymized analytics by enabling "Do Not Track" in your browser or by using a privacy-focused browser extension.
  • Consent withdrawal: If you previously consented to optional cookies, you can withdraw consent at any time by clearing your browser cookies for this site.

Browser-specific instructions:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

6. Your Consent

When you first visit the Site, you will see a cookie banner that allows you to accept or decline optional cookies. Strictly necessary cookies are always active because the Site cannot function without them.

You can change your consent choices at any time by clearing your browser cookies and refreshing the page, which will re-display the banner.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, regulation, or our practices. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

8. More Information

For more details about how we handle personal data, please see our Privacy Policy. If you have questions about this Cookie Policy, contact us at privacy@bravellecasalines.com.

This document is provided for transparency and does not constitute legal advice. It is intended to clearly describe our cookie practices in plain language.

About Bravelle Casa Lines

Bravelle Casa Lines is an independent editorial publication focused on architecture, interior design, and the built environment. We publish long-form articles, practical guides, and critical essays for people who care about how spaces are made — and how they feel to inhabit.

Our editorial approach is deliberately slow. We prefer depth over frequency, clarity over cleverness, and useful knowledge over trend reporting. Whether you are a practicing architect, a design student, or a homeowner planning a renovation, our aim is to give you something worth your time.

We are reader-supported and editorially independent. No sponsored content. No brand partnerships disguised as articles.
